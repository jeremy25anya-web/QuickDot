## The short version
QuickDot has no accounts. We do not store the text, links, images, or files you
share — they pass through our server in memory and are gone when your room closes
or expires. We collect a small amount of technical information (like IP
addresses) only to keep the service running and to prevent abuse.

## What we collect

**Content you share — not stored.** Messages and files are relayed between
participants in real time and held only briefly in server memory to complete
delivery. They are **never written to disk** and are discarded when the room
ends. We do not read, index, back up, or retain your content.

**Technical and abuse-prevention data — retained briefly.** To operate the
service and prevent misuse, we process:
- **IP addresses** — used for rate-limiting and abuse prevention. Under Canadian
  law an IP address can be personal information, so we disclose this plainly.
- **Connection metadata** — timestamps, room codes, and approximate
  participant/transfer counts.
- **File metadata** — a file's size, type, and a cryptographic hash (not its
  contents) may be recorded to respond to abuse reports.

We retain this technical data for up to **30 days**, then delete it, unless we
are legally required to keep it longer (for example, to respond to a lawful
request or a child-safety report).

**Analytics.** We use **Cloudflare Web Analytics**, which is privacy-first: it
uses **no cookies** and collects only aggregate, non-identifying usage data. We
do not build advertising profiles and we do not sell data.

**Error monitoring.** We use **Sentry** to receive reports when the app or our
server hits an error. A report contains the type of error and where in our code
it happened. Before a report leaves your browser or our server, we remove error
messages, room codes, IP addresses, and request details, and we have configured
Sentry not to store IP addresses. Sentry processes these reports in the United
States.

## What we do **not** collect
No names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords. No accounts. No stored message or
file content. No advertising trackers or third-party ad cookies.

## Why we process this data
We process the limited technical data above under our legitimate interest in
operating, securing, and preventing abuse of the service, consistent with
PIPEDA. Providing an IP address is inherent to using any website; if you do not
want it processed for these purposes, do not use the service.

## When we share data
We do not sell your data. We may disclose the limited technical data we hold:
- to comply with a valid legal request or court order;
- to meet child-safety reporting obligations required by Canadian law;
- to our infrastructure providers (Cloudflare, Fly.io) and our error-monitoring
  provider (Sentry) strictly to run the service.

## Where your data is processed
Our infrastructure providers may process data on servers **outside Canada**
(including the United States). By using QuickDot you understand your technical
data may be processed in other countries under their laws.

## Children
QuickDot is not directed to children under 13 and they should not use it. Sharing
child sexual abuse material is strictly prohibited, will be reported to the
appropriate authorities, and may be disclosed as required by law.

## Security
We enforce transport encryption (HTTPS/WSS), server-side limits on file size and
type, rate limiting, and metadata-only logging. No service is perfectly secure;
because content is ephemeral and unstored, the data at risk is deliberately
minimal.
